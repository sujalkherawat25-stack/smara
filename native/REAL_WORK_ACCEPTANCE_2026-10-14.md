HTTP502 path. Native retries then consumed additional conservative goal request
reservations, despite the outbound evaluation guard preventing extra spending.

the turn without exposing exception text or a private payload. Normal transient

relaxed. This fix is candidate source, not a retroactive change to v0.1.23.
