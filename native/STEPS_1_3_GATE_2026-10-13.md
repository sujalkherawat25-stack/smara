## Step 1 — reliability

- Shared cross-process provider admission, bounded concurrency/queueing and
  cancellation-aware admission. Provider keys/prompts are not stored in that
  ledger. Leases renew and expire after interruption.
- Preserve known model output allowances across foreground chat, goals and
  scheduled runs; expose explicit output, request and estimate ceilings.
- Run normal tools first; use at most one same-thread, tool-free checkpoint
  formatting repair, charged to the same cumulative budget.
- Isolate unexpected worker failures, retain project ownership until cleanup,
  and target a selected goal when explicitly continuing it.
- Re-read authoritative native evidence on resume. Interrupted summaries retain
  prior observations, revalidate output paths, and invalidate old test evidence
  after a new edit. Partial observations never certify goal completion.
- No automatic action replay after uncertain interruption. Existing conservative
  crash recovery/manual resume remains; instant cancellation of every operating
  system process and universal sandbox escape protection are not certified here.
