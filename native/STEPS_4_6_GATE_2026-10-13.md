  Desktop, CLI and Telegram. There is no additional scheduler/agent service.
- New schedules are paused. Prior occurrences must be accepted before the next
  wake; missed wakes coalesce rather than creating an accumulated backlog.
- Recurrence is initially read-only, finite, budgeted and without interactive
  browser origins. Uncertain/interrupted effects are not automatically replayed.

- Local Windows connectivity status makes offline waiting visible. This is not

- Login startup is explicit opt-in, launching the installed GUI-subsystem wrapper
  and hidden companion worker. No startup preference was enabled during testing;

## Step 6 — experimental scoped Windows control

- Off by default; foreground interactive sessions only, never scheduled/background
  goals. Initial allowlist: Notepad, Paint, Calculator in trusted install roots.
- The human selects the current window for 15 minutes / 40 actions. Every native
  observe/action requires approval. Grant selection/Allow/Deny were not automated.
- After native approval, a bounded 10-second handoff lets the human bring the app
  forward. Smara never activates windows itself. Stop/expiry and ownership are
  checked while waiting; ownership errors and UI actions are never retried.
- Fresh observation IDs, complete-state hashes, 30-second freshness, foreground,
  HWND/process/create-time checks and bounded UIA invoke/set-value only.
- No global keys, arbitrary coordinates, terminals, login/security dialogs or
  background/modal control. Screenshots render only the selected HWND, not a
  desktop crop; unsupported/black captures fail closed.
- Stop revokes the grant; an already-dispatched action can still have an uncertain
  result. Failed dispatch consumes its observation/allowance. Usage persists across
  reconnects and is reported from the durable grant.
- Source/frozen Windows imports and MCP initialization/listing passed without
  selecting or operating an app. Policy tests use explicitly synthetic backends.
  **Actual approved Windows capture/edit/invoke, human Deny, focus races and escape
  testing are not yet passed. General computer-use readiness is not claimed.**
