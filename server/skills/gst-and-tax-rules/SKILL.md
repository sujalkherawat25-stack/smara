# Answering a tax-rule question

Give the rule FIRST, in plain words, with the number of days / rate / consequence. Ask for their figures only after that. Say once that rates and dates change and the CA confirms the exact section. Never refuse because you "do not have their data": the rule does not need it.

## ITC and an unpaid supplier (CGST Rule 37), NOT 43B(h)
- If the buyer has not paid the supplier within **180 days of the invoice date**, the ITC taken on that invoice has to be **reversed** (added back in GSTR-3B) and **interest** (18% a year, section 50) is payable for the period the credit was taken and not paid for.
- When the supplier is paid later, the ITC can be **taken again** (re-availed) without further interest. Payment by credit note / set-off against what the supplier owes also counts.
- Reverse charge supplies and cases where the supplier is a related party but the payment is made are handled separately; the CA decides.
- 43B(h) is a different rule: an INCOME-TAX deduction for MSME (micro/small) suppliers not paid in 15 / 45 days. It does not cancel ITC.

## Blocked credit, section 17(5)
Motor vehicles for people (except some business uses), food, beverages, outdoor catering, club or health-club membership, beauty or cosmetic work, rent-a-cab, life and health insurance (with exceptions), employee travel benefits, works contract or construction of an immovable property for own use, goods lost/stolen/destroyed or given as gifts or free samples, personal use. The CA checks the exceptions.

## GSTR-3B set-off order
IGST credit pays IGST first, then CGST, then SGST. CGST credit pays CGST, then IGST (never SGST). SGST credit pays SGST, then IGST (never CGST). Credit cannot be used against interest or late fee: those are paid in cash.

## Usual dates (the government extends them from time to time: say "usually")
GSTR-1: 11th of the next month (quarterly filers: 13th after the quarter). GSTR-3B: 20th (QRMP: 22nd or 24th). TDS deposit: 7th of the next month (30 April for March). TDS returns: 31 July, 31 October, 31 January, 31 May. Advance tax: 15 June, 15 September, 15 December, 15 March.

## Interest and late fee in one line each
GST interest on late tax or wrongly used ITC: 18% a year. Late fee for GSTR-1/3B is a fixed amount per day with a cap, lower for nil returns. Use web_search for the current figure before quoting a rupee amount.
